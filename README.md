## How NoInUPI works

USSD lets you access banking services through your phone's dial pad without internet.
Traditional *99# USSD payments are a pain in the a*s. You dial codes, navigate menus, and enter details manually.
NoInUPI streamlines this process with a simple interface. Scan a QR code, enter the amount, and interact with your bank through USSD. To receive money, NoInUPI locally generates a QR code from your UPI ID.

## Features

- **Offline UPI payments** — Make payments through USSD banking without mobile data or Wi-Fi.
- **Send payments** — Scan a merchant's UPI QR code instead of entering their UPI ID manually and send money upto 5000 dabloons per each transaction.
- **Receive payments** — Generate a QR code locally from your UPI ID so others can scan and pay you.
- **USSD automation** — Simplifies the traditional dial-pad process through an Android interface.
- **Balance checking** — Check your bank balance through supported USSD banking services.
- **Transaction history** — View locally stored transaction records.
- **Payment status** — View payment results based on the available banking confirmation.
- **Local storage** — Keep transaction records on your device without a cloud database or a server and not even backend.
- **Clean retro UI** — A minimal, retro-inspired interface.
